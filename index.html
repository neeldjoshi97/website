FAQs and AI Hacks

1. Can I block LLMs from scraping my personal data? Yes, adding a robots.txt file to online directory can prevent (some ethical) LLM crawlers from accessing your data for training.

2. I am worried that my social media can be used to create deepfakes... Uploading slightly lower resolution images limits AI's ability to morph them. Also such deepfakes are less effective and can be easier to detect.

3. AI is already better than humans... Besides generative models that have truly disrupted creative arts, today's AI is simply better at predicting the next element in a sequence. It is far from being as intelligent as even a cat.

4. Will AI take over my job...? Short answer: Not right away. Long answer: AI is central to Industry 4.0, primarily automating routine tasks. Like previous industrial revolutions, it will change the nature of work eventually. For jobseekers, qualities like honesty, integrity, and adaptability will matter most. The best step now is to learn AI tools relevant to your profession to stay competitive.

5. How trustworthy is AI? As long as AI is probabilistic in nature, it will always err. Hence LLM apps come with disclaimers, like 'AI can make mistakes, check output for errors'.

Fun fact: this problem has given rise to an entire field called Responsible AI, which is trying to make AI more reliable than ever.

6. If I am not a computer scientist or a programmer, why should I be interested in AI? Although AI is a computer algorithm, like other algorithms, it has potential applications across every profession. Like computers, AI is becoming a common tool that every field will engage with and contribute to in its own way.

Fun fact: concept of AI has evolved from the field of Robotics which is a combination of mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering.
